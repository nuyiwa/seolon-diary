Seolon Diary Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 · 한국어

Information We Collect

Seolon Diary does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no server that receives or stores your records or personal information. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Where Your Records Are Stored

Everything you make in the app — handwriting, notes, journal, to-dos, photos, stickers — is stored only on your iPad. Deleting the app deletes these records too, so please keep a backup file with “Backup & Restore Records” in the app's Settings. Backup files are saved only where you choose (such as the Files app).

Permissions the App Asks For

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo to put in your diary.
  • Calendar: used to show, add and edit events in your diary. Events stay in your device's calendar and are never sent to the developer.
  • Photo picker: only the photos you choose are copied into your diary.

Internet Use

The app never sends your records over the internet. If you type an image URL for a book cover, the app connects to that server to show the image, and ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) may reach that server.

Purchases

Diary purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple's App Store. Apple manages payment information; it is not shared with the developer.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no one's personal information, including children's.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll let you know on this page and on the Privacy Policy screen in the app.
